# Privacy Policy for WeMi Switch Control

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

## Overview
WeMi Switch Control ("the App") is a local-network smart switch controller. We respect your privacy and have designed the App so that all remembered homes, discovered switches, preferences, and local backups remain strictly on your Android device, while native switch automation rules live directly on compatible smart switches over your local Wi-Fi network.

## Information Collection and Use
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The App does not require an account, login, email address, cloud registration, or Android GPS/Location permissions.
- **Local Network Communication Only:** The App uses standard Android Wi-Fi and network permissions (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`) solely to discover UPnP/SSDP smart switches on your local Wi-Fi network (`239.255.255.250:1900`), control switches (`GetBinaryState`, `SetBinaryState`), read, write, or synchronize on-device switch automation rules and switch clocks (`FetchRules`, `StoreRules`, `TimeSync`), and optionally transfer your household switch configuration between your own WeMi devices over local Wi-Fi.
- **On-Device Storage, Cloud Backup Exclusion & Diagnostics:** Remembered Wi-Fi home profiles and discovered switch inventory (names, local IP addresses, ports, and hardware metadata) are stored locally on your device using Android Room v7 SQLite storage alongside local `SharedPreferences` for Home Screen widget target bindings and app preferences. Automation rules are stored directly inside compatible smart switches' onboard memory rather than on any external server. Before modifying on-device rules, the App saves local pre-write raw-ZIP backups (`hardware_rules_backups`) so you can restore previous rule sets in one tap. All app data — Room database, `SharedPreferences`, local pre-write rule backups (`hardware_rules_backups`), and optional local diagnostic exports (`wemi-telemetry-latest.txt`) — is excluded from Android cloud Auto-Backup (`backup_rules.xml` and `data_extraction_rules.xml`). Direct device-to-device cable migration may transfer saved homes, switches, and widget bindings while continuing to exclude `hardware_rules_backups` and `wemi-telemetry-latest.txt`. Exported diagnostic reports redact Wi-Fi SSIDs, MAC addresses, serial numbers, UDNs, and DNS IPs by default unless you explicitly enable Full Diagnostics before exporting.
- **Optional Zero-Cloud Household Config Sync (User-Initiated Only):** You may optionally export or import your saved Wi-Fi homes, custom switch names, room assignments, sort order, local LAN IP addresses, ports, and switch hardware IDs (`serialNumber`/`UDN`) either as a user-selected JSON file via Android Storage Access Framework or directly to another WeMi device (such as Android TV) on your local Wi-Fi using a temporary 120-second one-time 6-digit PIN (`UDP 49190` discovery and SPAKE2/AES-256-GCM encrypted `HTTP :8990` transfer). Wi-Fi passwords are never stored, exported, or transferred, and no cloud server or intermediary is ever involved.
- **Optional Solar Location (Offline Presets or Manual Coordinates Only):** If you configure sunrise or sunset schedules on a compatible switch, you may select an offline built-in city preset or manually enter latitude and longitude values. Those coordinates are written solely over your local Wi-Fi network to your switch's onboard `LOCATIONINFO` table so the switch can calculate sunrise and sunset locally. The App requests zero Android Location permissions, never accesses GPS or network location, and never transmits coordinates to external servers or includes exact coordinates in exported telemetry reports.

## Third-Party Sharing & Analytics
The App contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers, and does not sell or transmit user data to any external server.

## Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or local UPnP switch compatibility, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.
