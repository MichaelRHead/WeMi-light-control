**WeMi Light Control** (`org.suppressingfire.wemi` / `com.aistudio.wemiswitch.kxpqzr`, `v23.0`) is an Android & Android TV local-LAN controller for Belkin's discontinued smart switch platform (switches, dimmers, and mini plugs). ## 1. Supported Hardware (Belkin's Discontinued Smart Switch Platform)

The app supports four physical hardware families from Belkin's discontinued smart switch platform:

1. **In-Wall Light Switch (1st Gen)** (`F7C030`, UPnP `LightSwitch` / `uuid:Lightswitch-1_0-...`):
   - Single-pole in-wall paddle switch with green Wi-Fi/power LED indicator and binary relay (`0`/`1`).
2. **In-Wall 3-Way Light Switch (2nd Gen RTOS)** (`WLS040` / `WLS0403`, UPnP `LightSwitch` / `uuid:Lightswitch-2_0-...` / `3_0`):
   - 3-way compatible in-wall rocker switch supporting multi-location traveler switching and binary relay (`0`/`1`), protected by `Semaphore(3)` LAN concurrency limiting.
3. **In-Wall Wi-Fi Smart Dimmer** (`F7C059` OpenWrt & `WDS060` RTOS, UPnP `Dimmer`):
   - In-wall touch dimmer with 7-segment green LED brightness bar, continuous `1–100%` dimming (`<brightness>`), and hardware Night Mode (`20%` soft cap via `ConfigureNightMode`).
4. **Mini Smart Plug** (`F7C063` & `WSP080`, UPnP `Socket` / `Mini`):
   - Compact horizontal pass-through smart outlet plug with side power toggle and front LED status indicator.

## 2. Architecture & Source Layout

### Package Structure (`app/src/main/java/org/suppressingfire/wemi/`)

- **`MainActivity.kt` & `ui/components/WeMiModalDialogs.kt`**:
  - Entry point (`WeMiSwitchApp`, `348L`). Automatically detects Android TV (`UI_MODE_TYPE_TELEVISION`), renders the auto-detected `10.22.22.x` **Switch Setup AP Banner**, and delegates mobile/tablet modal hosting (`ProvisionApDialog`, `AddOrEditDeviceDialog`, `SsdpScannerModal`, `AddWifiHomeNetworkDialog`, `AddRuleDialog`) to `WeMiModalDialogs.kt`.
- **`data/` (Room v5 Persistence, Self-Healing Repository & Scheduler)**:
  - `DeviceEntities.kt`:
    - `DeviceType` (`SWITCH_PLUG`, `MOTION_SENSOR`, `LIGHTBULB_DIMMER`), `RuleType` (`SCHEDULE`, `MOTION_TRIGGER`, `AUTO_OFF`), and `DeviceCategory` (`classifyDeviceCategory`: `CHRISTMAS_TREE`, `FAN`, `LIGHT`, `MOTION_SENSOR`, `SWITCH_PLUG`).
    - `normalizeDeviceNameForInference`, `inferRoomFromName` & `resolveDeviceRoomAssignment`: Normalizes CamelCase/delimiters, infers rooms in specificity order (`"Ensuite"` before `"Bedroom"`, `"bed"`/`"bedside"`/`"nightstand"`/`"girls"`/`"boys"`/`"kids"` → `"Bedroom"`, `"stair"`/`"stairway"` → `"Hallway & Landing"`, word-bounded `"den"`/`"hall"`, and `"Unassigned"` fallback instead of `"Living Room"`), and non-destructively migrates switches stuck in `"Living Room"`.
    - `DeviceEntity.matchesDiscovered` & `SsdpDiscoveredDevice.matchesEndpointOrSerial`: Canonical 3-tier hardware identity and discovery deduplication helpers shared across `DiscoveryCoordinator`, `SsdpDiscoveryEngine`, and `SsdpScannerModal`.
    - `ConnectionQuality`: `EXCELLENT` (<= 28 ms, 4 bars), `GOOD` (<= 65 ms, 3 bars), `SLOW` (> 65 ms, 2 bars), `OFFLINE` (0 bars).
    - `HomeNetworkEntity` (`wemo_home_networks`), `DeviceEntity` (`wemo_devices`), and `RuleEntity` (`wemo_rules`).
  - `SwitchDao.kt` & `SwitchDatabase.kt`: Room database (`wemi_switch_database`, schema `version = 5`, per-`Application` in-memory isolation under Robolectric).
  - `SwitchRepository.kt`: Public repository facade holding `Semaphore(3)` RTOS protection, per-device `Mutex`, unified `executeOptimisticDeviceCommand` (optimistic UI write, `lastSetStateTimestampMs` GENA echo suppression, 2-stage reconnect, and offline revert), startup `ensureSeeded`, one-tap `resetLanHomesSwitchesAndRooms()`, and UPnP GENA push / 30s fallback polling.
  - `DiscoveryCoordinator.kt`: 3-tier hardware identity matcher (`matchesDiscoveredDevice` & `findMatchingExistingDevice`), startup `GetBinaryState` detection (`detectStartupDeviceStates`), startup soft-AP SSID sanitization + legacy `"Living Room"` room re-inference (`sanitizeAndResolveInitialLanHome`), 2-stage self-healing reconnect (`attemptTwoStageReconnect`), SSDP scan & auto-adoption (`executeSsdpScan`, `adoptDiscoveredDevice`), and zero-permission subnet/SSID home sync with `isWemoDeviceSetupSsid` filtering.
  - `ProvisioningCoordinator.kt`: `10.22.22.1` Setup AP probing (`probeSetupApDevice`), 2.4 GHz AP scanning (`scanSetupApNetworks`), `WiFiSetup1#ConnectHomeNetwork` provisioning (`provisionSetupApDevice`), Demo Showroom AP simulation, and post-provisioning Room registration.
  - `DeviceCrudAndRulesCoordinator.kt`: Room v5 CRUD, manual-IP 9-port hardware enrichment (`saveDevice`), NVRAM `ChangeFriendlyName` sync, room batch power/brightness fan-out, and local `RuleEntity` execution.
  - `DemoFixtures.kt`: Showcase Demo Showroom fixtures (`DEFAULT_ROOMS`, `defaultHomeNetworks`, `defaultClassicDevices` — `14` showcase devices across `3` Wi-Fi Homes — and `defaultClassicRules`).
  - `RuleScheduler.kt`: Coroutine engine for per-device/rule Auto-Off countdowns (`activeCountdownsSec`) and daily `SCHEDULE` / Sunset / Sunrise rule execution.
- **`network/` (Zero-Dependency Local UPnP / SSDP / SOAP / GENA & Provisioning Engine)**:
  - `LanHttpClient.kt`: Lightweight zero-dependency HTTP/1.1 & UPnP GENA (`GET`, `POST`, `SUBSCRIBE`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`) client built directly on `SocketFactory` with byte-exact `Content-Length` / chunked framing.
  - `LanRuntimeUtils.kt` & `NetworkModels.kt`: Shared zero-dependency placeholder-serial constants (`PLACEHOLDER_SERIALS`, `isPlaceholderSerial`), null-safe `isRunningInRobolectric()`, `wlan*`-prioritized `resolvePreferredLanInterfaceIpv4()`, and shared wire DTOs (`SoapCallResult`, `SwitchMetaInfo`, `SsdpScanReport`, `ScannedAp`, `SetupDeviceDescriptor`, `ProvisionResult`, `ParsedBinaryState`).
  - `NetworkClient.kt`:
    - 9-port `probeWemoPort` (`49153, 49152, 49154, 49151, 49155..49159`) with TCP connect timeout vs. `ECONNREFUSED` vs. read timeout separation and `<serialNumber>`/`<UDN>` verification.
    - Unified `executeWithPortHealing` (with Wi-Fi bound `wifiSocketFactory` candidate fallback), `setBinaryState` (with `<BinaryState>Error</BinaryState>` handling), `getBinaryState`, `getMetaInfo`, `getSignalStrength`, `changeFriendlyName`, `configureNightMode`, and companion delegations to `XmlAndSkuParser` and `SsdpDiscoveryEngine`.
  - `XmlAndSkuParser.kt`: Stateless UPnP/SSDP/SOAP builders and parsers (`buildSoapEnvelope`, `soapActionHeader`, `escapeXml`, `parseBinaryStatePayload`, `buildSsdpMSearchPacket`, `parseSsdpHeaders`, `classifyHardwareSku`, `parseSetupXmlDescriptor`, `parseMetaInfoSoapResponse` with Belkin soft-AP `WeMo.*` / `Wemo.LS3W.*` SSID filtering, `parseSignalStrengthSoapResponse`, `parseApListXml`).

  - `NetworkMonitor.kt`: Zero-Location-permission `ConnectivityManager.NetworkCallback` `/24` subnet & `10.22.22.x` Setup AP detector with `createWifiBoundSocketFactory` (caching unvalidated `NetworkCallback` Wi-Fi handles + loopback `127.0.0.1` & Robolectric aware).
